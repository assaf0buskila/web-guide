# Before / After Optimization Examples

Real-world patterns showing how to transform content across all five layers. Each example shows the original (❌) and optimized (✅) version with annotations explaining which layers benefit.

---

## Example 1: Opening Paragraph of a Blog Post

### ❌ Before
> In today's fast-paced digital landscape, businesses need to stay ahead of the curve. Search engine optimization has been around for a long time, and it's more important than ever. Let's dive in and explore some tips that can help you improve your website's performance and visibility.

**Problems:**
- No extractable answer (AEO ❌)
- No data points (GEO ❌)
- Vague — could be about anything (AIO ❌)
- Cliché opening wastes the most important real estate (SEO ❌)
- No clear value for the reader (SXO ❌)

### ✅ After
> Search engine optimization (SEO) is the practice of improving a website's visibility in organic search results through technical, content, and authority improvements. In 2026, SEO works alongside four additional optimization layers — AEO, GEO, SXO, and AIO — to ensure content is discoverable in traditional search, extractable by answer engines, citable by AI systems, and optimized for user experience. This guide covers all five layers with actionable strategies for each.

**Improvements:**
- Opens with a clean, extractable definition (AEO ✅)
- Names all five entities explicitly (AIO ✅)
- Sets clear expectation for the reader (SXO ✅)
- Primary keyword "search engine optimization" in first sentence (SEO ✅)
- Structured claim AI can cite (GEO ✅)

## Example 2: Product Description

### ❌ Before
> Our amazing tool helps businesses do more with less. It's incredibly fast, super easy to use, and loved by thousands of companies worldwide. Try it today and see the difference!

**Problems:**
- No entity name in opening (AIO ❌)
- No specific claims — all superlatives (GEO ❌)
- Not extractable — "our tool" has no meaning out of context (AEO ❌)
- No evidence or social proof (SXO ❌)

### ✅ After
> DataSync Pro is a real-time data integration platform that connects over 200 SaaS applications without requiring custom code. DataSync Pro processes an average of 2.4 million records per hour with 99.97% uptime, serving 3,400+ businesses including Shopify, Stripe, and HubSpot. The platform offers three pricing tiers starting at $49/month, with a 14-day free trial.

**Improvements:**
- Entity named and defined in first sentence (AIO ✅, AEO ✅)
- Specific, verifiable numbers (GEO ✅)
- Named customers as social proof (SXO ✅)
- Includes pricing and trial info — high-value for AI product queries (AEO ✅, GEO ✅)
- Primary keyword "data integration platform" in definition (SEO ✅)

## Example 3: FAQ Answer

### ❌ Before
> **Q: How much does it cost?**
> A: It depends on your needs. We have several different options available. Please contact our sales team for a custom quote that's right for you.

**Problems:**
- Avoids answering the actual question (AEO ❌)
- Nothing extractable by any system (GEO ❌, AIO ❌)
- Friction — forces user to contact sales for basic info (SXO ❌)

### ✅ After
> **Q: How much does DataSync Pro cost?**
> A: DataSync Pro offers three pricing tiers: Starter at $49/month (up to 50,000 records), Professional at $149/month (up to 500,000 records), and Enterprise at custom pricing for unlimited records. All plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. Annual billing saves 20% on Starter and Professional plans.

**Improvements:**
- Directly answers the question with specific numbers (AEO ✅)
- Self-contained — works perfectly as a standalone answer (AEO ✅, AIO ✅)
- Entity named in both Q and A (AIO ✅)
- Specific, citable pricing data (GEO ✅)
- Reduces friction by providing the answer upfront (SXO ✅)

## Example 5: Comparison Content

### ❌ Before
> DataSync Pro is better than the competition in many ways. While other tools may struggle with large datasets, our platform handles them easily. We also have better customer support and more integrations than most alternatives.

**Problems:**
- No named competitors (AIO ❌)
- No specific comparisons (GEO ❌)
- Subjective claims without evidence (AEO ❌)

### ✅ After

> | Feature | DataSync Pro | Zapier | Make (Integromat) |
> |---------|-------------|--------|-------------------|
> | Native integrations | 200+ | 6,000+ | 1,500+ |
> | Records per hour | 2.4M | 150K | 300K |
> | Real-time sync | Yes | No (polling) | Yes |
> | Starting price | $49/mo | $29.99/mo | $10.59/mo |
> | Uptime SLA | 99.97% | 99.9% | 99.9% |
>
> DataSync Pro processes data at 16x the speed of Zapier and 8x the speed of Make, making it the strongest choice for high-volume data operations. Zapier offers the broadest integration catalog, while Make provides the lowest entry price for smaller workloads.

**Improvements:**
- Table format is natively parseable by AI (AIO ✅)
- Named competitors with specific data (GEO ✅)
- AI can extract comparative claims directly (AEO ✅)
- Balanced comparison builds trust (SXO ✅, E-E-A-T)
- Long-tail comparison keywords ("DataSync Pro vs Zapier") (SEO ✅)
- Summary below table provides extractable comparison sentences (AEO ✅, GEO ✅)

## Example 6: Call to Action

### ✅ After

**Improvements:**
- Specific value proposition with time and friction reduction (SXO ✅)

## Example 8: Statistics Without and With Attribution

### ❌ Before
> Studies show that most businesses see better results when they optimize for AI search.

**Problems:**
- "Studies show" is unverifiable (GEO ❌)
- "Most businesses" and "better results" are vague (AEO ❌)
- No source to cross-reference (AIO ❌)

### ✅ After
> Brands that have invested in AI search optimization achieve 9x higher visibility in AI-generated responses compared to brands that have not, according to an analysis of 500+ brands by Erlin in early 2026.

**Improvements:**
- Specific multiplier with source and date (GEO ✅)
- Self-contained, citable sentence (AEO ✅)
- Named source allows AI to cross-reference (AIO ✅)
- Compelling data encourages the reader to keep reading (SXO ✅)

## Pattern Summary

The most common transformations that improve content across multiple layers simultaneously:

1. **Name entities explicitly** → improves AIO, AEO, GEO, SEO
2. **Replace vague claims with specific data** → improves GEO, AEO, SXO
3. **Use the definition-lead pattern** → improves AEO, AIO, GEO
4. **Add comparison tables** → improves AIO, GEO, AEO, SEO
5. **Write self-contained sections** → improves AEO, AIO, GEO
6. **Include attribution and dates** → improves GEO, E-E-A-T, AIO
7. **Use descriptive headings** → improves SEO, SXO, AIO
8. **Lead with the answer** → improves AEO, SXO
